The adapters use ComfyUI/Diffusers model graphs and an independent AMD Triton
packed-weight backend. They do not load the NVIDIA Nunchaku extension.
LTX encoder/connectors use independent NF4 weight-only decoding; their NF4
weights are dependencies, not SVDQ transformer projections.
Existing upstream license texts remain bundled. Downloaded models and their
components retain their respective upstream licenses and use restrictions.
NVIDIA numerical parity is unverified.
