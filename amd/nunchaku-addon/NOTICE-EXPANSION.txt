Independent AMD adapters for published Nunchaku INT4 checkpoints.
No NVIDIA Nunchaku extension is bundled or emulated.

FLUX.1 Schnell checkpoint: nunchaku-tech/nunchaku-flux.1-schnell
revision b18e8e6333da55835f6ce16c04b13c543c0954b1.
Base model Black Forest Labs FLUX.1 Schnell; model card Apache 2.0.
https://huggingface.co/nunchaku-tech/nunchaku-flux.1-schnell

Z-Image Turbo checkpoint: nunchaku-tech/nunchaku-z-image-turbo
revision ca6bac69c3b0b2bdd31ca5196bf87c5f2a9eaedf.
Separate signed INT4 and E2M1 FP4/FP8-scale variants are supported.
https://huggingface.co/nunchaku-tech/nunchaku-z-image-turbo
Encoder/VAE: Comfy-Org/z_image_turbo
revision 6fc90a3b1b653e935a0d175e260736de25b84df5.
Model weights retain their original licenses and are downloaded separately.

Packing and graph-layout references: Nunchaku/SVDQuant and DeepCompressor
(MIT HAN Lab and contributors), Apache 2.0; nunchaku-lite adapters
https://github.com/rootonchair/nunchaku-lite (Apache 2.0).
Reference revision e663e48ccf3ec5264355e1872aeebef0284706c9.
The AMD adapters use the existing ComfyUI implementation of model graphs,
attention and positional embeddings; ComfyUI retains its original license.
The included example image was generated locally to demonstrate editing.
