Qwen 2.1 checkpoint format and references

This independently authored AMD loader reads the Nunchaku SVDQ signed INT4
checkpoint published by MesmerTech, revision
868fc8b147de20f2c6e6835b694b2c4d9de9b5a6:
https://huggingface.co/mesmertech/Mesmer-Image-21-Nunchaku
The checkpoint uses upstream Nunchaku/DeepCompressor packing, signed W4A4
projections and BF16 rank-128 compensation branches. Model weights are not
included in this installer. They retain their own Qwen Research License.

Turbo uses the Viggle v0.3 six-step adapter at revision
009a44a895ef85f7e643c80fdca9543795248867:
https://huggingface.co/Viggle/Qwen-Image-2.1-viggle-turbo
Its runtime LoRA and resolution-dependent schedule follow the published
method, implemented here for the AMD INT4 modules. Adapter weights retain
their separate model license and are not bundled.

ComfyUI provides the Qwen 2.1 transformer architecture, positional encoding,
shared modulation, attention and sampling. The 224 block projection layers
are replaced by this project's AMD packed INT4 backend. This does not run
the official NVIDIA Nunchaku extension, native INT4 matrix instructions,
or NVFP4 kernels. AMD numerical equivalence to NVIDIA is unverified.
